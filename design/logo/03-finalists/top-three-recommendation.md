# Top Three Recommendation

## 1. Concept 09 — Low Horizon

**Why it leads:** It has the strongest small-size performance, the clearest outer kaf silhouette, and the most efficient proportion beside the long `Eliran Cohen` name. The contained counter remains open at 16 px and avoids a Latin `X`.

**Risk:** Its low horizontal posture can feel more product/technology than boutique advisory. The next round should introduce slightly more editorial tension without sacrificing the aperture.

## 2. Concept 01 — Countercut

**Why it remains strategically important:** It best expresses quiet premium authority. The curved kaf is calm and cultivated, while the aleph is discovered inside rather than announced as a literal monogram. If the brief's 45% premium-editorial priority is decisive, this is the creative-director recommendation despite placing second numerically.

**Risk:** At 16–24 px the aleph becomes a compact counter cluster. The next round must simplify the three negative strokes and widen their junction.

## 3. Concept 05 — Monolith

**Why it belongs:** It is the most technological and favicon-ready option, with a stable compact silhouette and a clear systems/precision tone. The angular chamber also separates it from the rounded-category norm.

**Risk:** It can drift toward cybersecurity, infrastructure, or industrial software. Refinement must soften one or two corners and add optical asymmetry so it feels authored rather than modular.

## Recommendation to the user

- Choose **09** for the strongest functional digital system.
- Choose **01** for the strongest premium advisory positioning.
- Choose **05** for the strongest technology-forward positioning.

No typography, master artwork, final lockup, or production export should begin until one of these architectures is selected.
