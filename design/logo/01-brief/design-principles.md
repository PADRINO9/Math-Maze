# Eliran Cohen — Design Principles

## Brand promise

Eliran Cohen turns complex AI systems into clear, accountable business outcomes with cultivated judgment and precise execution.

## Locked identity

- Primary name: `Eliran Cohen`
- Descriptor: `AI SOLUTIONS`
- Category: premium B2B AI consultancy
- Core symbol brief: one inseparable abstract form built from a printed Hebrew `א` and a printed Hebrew `כ`
- Reading order: abstract premium symbol first; Hebrew construction discovered second
- Aesthetic balance: 45% premium editorial restraint, 35% technological precision, 20% Hebrew character
- Primary operating mode: pure black on white

## Desired signals

1. Cultivated authority without luxury-fashion mannerisms
2. Intelligent precision and command of complex systems
3. Personal ownership and senior advisory judgment
4. Contemporary B2B credibility
5. Timeless restraint with a distinctly Hebrew foundation

## Rejected signals

1. Generic AI, neural-network, circuit, chatbot, spark, or robot imagery
2. Latin `C`, `K`, `X`, `CK`, or `EC` monograms
3. Winery, legal, heraldic, crest, or fashion-house category drift
4. Meaning that depends on gradients, shadows, color, animation, or mockup context
5. Fragile hairlines, decorative complexity, or details that disappear at small sizes

## Symbol architecture

- The `כ` is a standard printed Hebrew kaf: a substantial upper and right-hand body whose aperture opens to the left.
- The `א` uses a dominant diagonal running from upper-left to lower-right, with its two secondary branches implied through shared mass, counterform, or controlled junctions.
- The two letters must share structure. Placing two recognizable glyphs side by side is not sufficient.
- The silhouette should remain coherent when the internal Hebrew reading is not yet noticed.
- The form must not produce an accidental Latin `X`, `K`, or `C` as its primary thumbnail reading.
- Each concept should rely on roughly 3–7 primary shapes or equivalent structural decisions.

## Controlled correction to the supplied reference

One construction note described a standard printed `כ` as opening to the right. That is anatomically incorrect: a standard printed `כ` opens to the left. The higher-level brief requires genuine Hebrew integrity, so all concepts use the standard left-facing aperture. This is treated as a factual reference correction, not as a change to the creative brief.

## Typographic register for the later wordmark phase

- English name: refined contemporary serif or serif-informed display construction; high authority, moderate contrast, no fashion-house exaggeration
- Descriptor: restrained uppercase sans serif with measured tracking
- Relationship: symbol leads; name supports; descriptor remains clearly tertiary
- No external font files or live-font dependencies will be introduced during concept exploration

## Application hierarchy

1. Primary signature: symbol + `Eliran Cohen`
2. Endorsed signature: symbol + name + `AI SOLUTIONS`
3. Standalone symbol: favicon, avatar, social mark, presentation corner, watermark
4. Small-size fallback: optically simplified symbol only

## Measurable pass/fail criteria

A concept passes only when all of the following are true:

- A Hebrew-literate viewer can discover both `א` and `כ` in the geometry without a diagram.
- The first thumbnail read is an abstract premium mark, not a Latin monogram.
- The silhouette remains identifiable at 24 px and structurally intact at 16 px.
- Critical gaps remain open at 16 px; no essential feature depends on a hairline.
- It works in one flat color and in reverse.
- It remains credible without the wordmark, descriptor, mockup, or explanatory copy.
- It contains no generic AI motif and no wine, crest, shield, or heraldic cue.
- It can be described and roughly sketched from memory in one sentence.
- The SVG contains no scripts, event handlers, external resources, remote fonts, images, `foreignObject`, DTD, or entity declarations.

## Phase boundary

This exploration ends after twelve black-and-white symbols are scored and three finalists are selected. Typography, optical refinement, master artwork, lockups, and export production begin only after the user chooses a direction.
