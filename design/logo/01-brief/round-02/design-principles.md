# Eliran Cohen — Locked Design Principles, Round 02

## Brand promise

Eliran Cohen turns complex AI ambiguity into clear, accountable business outcomes through cultivated judgment and technical precision.

## Five desired signals

1. Cultivated authority
2. Intelligent precision
3. Personal ownership
4. Modern B2B credibility
5. Timeless restraint

## Five rejected signals

1. Generic AI, cyber, circuit, robot, neural-network, or startup symbolism
2. Latin `C`, `K`, `X`, `CK`, or `EC` as the first read
3. Fashion-house, winery, legal, crest, seal, or heraldic category drift
4. Decorative or effect-dependent construction
5. Fragile calligraphy, hairlines, or detail that disappears below 24 px

## Symbol architecture

- One inseparable printed-Hebrew monogram
- Conceptual `כ` occupies the left structural field
- Conceptual `א` occupies the right decision field
- The `כ` supplies enclosure, counter, curve, or return
- The `א` supplies a dominant diagonal and two subordinate implied arms
- Both letters must be discoverable structurally, while the first read remains an abstract premium symbol
- Approximately 3–7 primary shapes per concept

## Anatomical correction

The construction reference contains a factual contradiction: it asks for standard printed `כ`, but describes its aperture as opening right. A locally rendered printed-Hebrew anatomy reference confirms the standard `כ` opens left. The higher-level requirement for a genuine standard `כ` therefore governs. Its conceptual field remains on the left, as explicitly requested.

## Typographic register for the later phase

- `Eliran Cohen`: restrained contemporary serif, flared sans, or serif–grotesk hybrid
- `AI SOLUTIONS`: uppercase modern sans, clearly secondary
- No remote font, downloaded font, or live-font dependency will be introduced
- Typography and lockups remain out of scope until a symbol is selected

## Application hierarchy

1. Primary horizontal signature
2. Stacked signature
3. Symbol-only mark
4. Favicon-grade simplified symbol
5. Wordmark-only fallback

## Measurable pass/fail criteria

A direction passes only if:

- `כ` is structurally present on the conceptual left and `א` on the conceptual right
- it does not become a dominant Latin `X`, `K`, or `C` at thumbnail size
- the silhouette is memorable with counters mentally filled
- all critical gaps remain open at 24 px
- the essential gesture survives at 16 px
- it works in pure black and pure white reverse
- it uses no hairline thinner than one quarter of the symbol width relationship
- it needs no color, text, effect, mockup, or explanation to feel intentional
- it can be sketched from memory in one sentence
- its SVG is static, local, self-contained, and valid XML

## Phase boundary

Round 02 ends after twelve symbol concepts, concise rationales, a weighted score matrix, and a top-three recommendation. No wordmark, descriptor, lockup, final master, or production export is authorized before user selection.
