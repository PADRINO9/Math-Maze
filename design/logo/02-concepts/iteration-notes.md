# Iteration Notes

## Round 1

The first rendered board was rejected as a finalist source. Although the left-opening `כ` structure was present, too many concepts produced a dominant Latin `X` or `K` reading at thumbnail size. Concepts 02, 06, and 11 also created unnecessarily busy counter intersections.

Per the non-overwrite policy, the first SVGs remain intact as an audit trail. The refined exploration uses `-v2` filenames and a `concept-board-v2.svg`.

## Round 2 objective

- Let the `כ` control the outer silhouette.
- Reveal the `א` primarily through counterform, interruption, or a three-way junction instead of a literal crossing.
- Reduce the number of independent diagonals.
- Widen critical gaps for 16–24 px use.
- Increase structural variety between concepts rather than changing only terminals or curvature.
