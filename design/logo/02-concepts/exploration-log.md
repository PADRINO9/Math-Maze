## Reduction logic

The twelve `v2` concepts represent distinct combinations of counter strategy, proportion, curvature, continuity, and junction logic. After rendering, the strongest general mechanism was #34: preserve the `כ` as the outer silhouette and contain the `א` inside it. That mechanism prevents the diagonal construction from becoming a Latin `X` and protects the symbol at small sizes.
