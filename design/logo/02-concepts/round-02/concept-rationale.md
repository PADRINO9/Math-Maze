# Round 02 — Concise Concept Rationale

The active comparison is `concept-board-v3.svg`. Because existing files may not be overwritten, the active forms for 06 and 10 are `concept-06-v2.svg` and `concept-10-v2.svg`; their first attempts remain as an audit trail.

## 01 — Shared Shoulder

The broad `כ` curve and the `א` meet at one diamond junction, expressing complexity resolving into one decision. It demonstrates the intended curve-versus-diagonal tension, but the positive crossing becomes a dominant Latin `X`; rejected.

## 02 — Contained Counter

A calm black `כ` controls the silhouette while three restrained cuts reveal the `א` on the conceptual right. The mark feels abstract first, Hebrew second, and remains coherent at favicon scale; finalist.

## 03 — Negative Kaf Field

The `כ` appears as a white counter inside a solid left field while a positive `א` grows from the right edge. The reversal is conceptually intelligent, but the outer read moves too close to a Latin `K`; rejected.

## 04 — Continuous Band

A single curved band changes direction into the aleph decision stroke, creating a fluid one-form construction. At thumbnail scale the diagonals equalize and form an `X`; rejected.

## 05 — Architectural Junction

Planar top and bottom kaf fields lock around one precise junction with the aleph diagonal. The construction is technological and memorable, but it reads as an angular `X` and drifts toward gaming/cyber; rejected.

## 06 — Tall Editorial Counter

A tall, softly bracketed `כ` houses a contained negative `א`, combining editorial proportion with technical counter control. It has the strongest premium tone and retains clean gaps through 16 px; finalist.

## 07 — Open Interlock

Separate kaf shoulders are made inseparable by the aleph diagonal and a central counter. The fusion is structurally clear, but the first read remains `X/K`; rejected.

## 08 — Compact Monolith

A dense, square-leaning `כ` contains an aleph cut and a small positive lock at the junction. It is the strongest favicon and technology-forward direction, with the central lock implying strategic intervention; finalist.

## 09 — Editorial Asymmetry

A lighter kaf curve is offset against a tall decisive aleph for cultivated tension. The proportions are elegant, but the positive diagonals create fashion-monogram and `X` associations; rejected.

## 10 — Counter Bridge

Upper and lower kaf shoulders bridge around an aleph-shaped negative intervention without a positive crossing. It avoids a Latin `X`, but the counter is still busier than the three finalists and loses clarity before they do; hold.

## 11 — One-Contour Lock

One continuous black contour creates the kaf return, aleph junction, and right terminal in a single silhouette. The gesture is memorable, but its dominant silhouette is an `X`; rejected.

## 12 — Stepped Resolution

Modular kaf planes and a decisive diagonal communicate systems and execution. It has strong technological credibility, but the result feels cyber-industrial and remains too close to `X/K`; rejected.
